How to read this table
Delivery plan
Draft plans need approval. “Not defined” means the specification does not provide a finite list of required product units.
Required product units
The number of units required by the plan. Draft numbers are provisional. For products with several release plans, a unit required in two plans counts twice.
Accepted coverage
Units with a published, accepted delivery out of the approved total. Repeat uploads do not increase coverage. Percentages appear when every release plan is approved; a competing upload does not revoke an accepted delivery.
